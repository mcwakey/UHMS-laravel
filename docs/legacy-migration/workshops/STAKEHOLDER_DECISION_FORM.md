# Stakeholder decision form

> Governance note (2026-07-21): the project owner resolved all 36 Phase 1B controlled outcomes by direct final-authority decision. This blank reusable form was not used as evidence of a workshop, signature, quorum, or approval and does not override the completed pack outcomes.

## Use and authority

This reusable form records one explicit stakeholder decision. Recommendations and facilitator notes are not approvals. Leave fields pending until the named authority supplies the outcome. Attach or cite signed minutes, an approved decision log, or an equivalent governance record before changing the decision status elsewhere.
